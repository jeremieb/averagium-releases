# 1.0.26

****

**Added**
- Status for features, tasks, and bugs: Backlog, In Progress, or Complete. Set it when creating or editing an item, or from the new Status menu in the actions menu of any feature, task, or bug. Status appears as a badge on each row. Progress works as before, and the two stay in step: reaching 100% completes an item, starting work moves it out of the backlog, and marking it complete sets it to 100%.
- Kanban board in the sidebar under Workspace, showing the features and bugs of all your projects in Backlog, In Progress, and Complete columns. Drag a card to another column, or right-click it and choose Move to. Backlog is ordered by due date, then start date, then priority, then creation order.
- Kanban tab in every project. A new Overview | Kanban switch under the project header shows the board for just that project.
- Card details. Click a card to open it in a full window with its comments, priority, dates, progress, and every task. Change a task's status, edit the item, or mark it complete right from there.

**Improved**
- Existing projects need no changes: items without a status get one from their progress (0% is Backlog, 1–99% is In Progress, 100% is Complete).
- The default AGENTS.md template now documents the `status:` field, so coding agents keep it up to date in your Markdown files.
